Ashen Steppe: Privacy Policy

Developer: Gantulga Khurelbaatar, Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location.
  • Your progress (silver, upgrades, heroes, settings, best times, daily gift streak) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason the game connects to the internet, and AdMob collects the device data described below.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not run our own analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (language, sound, graphics, left-handed layout, reduced effects and similar choices) are saved in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose "Erase all progress" in Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive or to double a reward) and occasional full-screen ads between fights. To deliver, measure and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location);
  • advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers;
  • interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches and ad views);
  • diagnostic information (such as crash logs and performance data).

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The ad content shown is limited to material suitable for teens (maximum ad content rating: Teen). Google may process this data in countries other than your own.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): on first launch the app shows a consent message. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
  • iOS: the app asks permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking. You can decline, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The game plays the same either way; ads then are not personalized.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Privacy (or Google → Ads).
  • Delete local data: Settings → Erase all progress, or uninstall the app.

Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, ad personalization and the use of device identifiers on your device rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Because we do not hold personal data about you, we cannot look up, export or delete anything on our side; for data held by Google, you can use the controls at myaccount.google.com. You may also contact us with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Ashen Steppe is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google as described above, no data leaves your device.

Security and retention

Data exchanged with the ad provider is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Retention of advertising data is governed by Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Төхөөрөмж дээр хадгалагдах мэдээлэл

Тоглоомын ахиц, тохиргоо (хэл, дуу, график, зүүн гарын байрлал, багасгасан эффект гэх мэт) апп-ын дотоод санд хадгалагдана. Энэ мэдээлэл таны төхөөрөмжөөс гардаггүй. Апп-аа устгах эсвэл Тохиргоо → «Бүх ахицыг устгах» дарвал бүрэн устна.

Реклам (Google AdMob)

Тоглоом Google AdMob-оор сонгох боломжтой шагналт реклам (жишээ нь босох, шагналаа хоёр дахин авах), мөн тулаан хооронд үе үе бүтэн дэлгэцийн реклам үзүүлдэг. Рекламыг хүргэх, хэмжих, залилангаас хамгаалах зорилгоор Google Mobile Ads SDK дараах мэдээллийг цуглуулж боловсруулж болно:

  • IP хаяг (ойролцоо байршлыг тооцоолоход);
  • рекламын танигч (Android Advertising ID, iOS дээр зөвшөөрсөн бол IDFA) болон төхөөрөмжийн бусад танигч;
  • рекламтай болон апптай харилцсан байдал (апп нээсэн, реклам үзсэн гэх мэт);
  • оношилгооны мэдээлэл (гацалтын тайлан, гүйцэтгэлийн мэдээлэл).

Энэ мэдээллийг Google өөрийн бодлогын дагуу боловсруулна: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, Google-ийн нууцлалын бодлого. Үзүүлэх рекламын агуулга өсвөр насныханд тохирсон түвшинд (Teen) хязгаарлагдсан. Google мэдээллийг таны оршин суугаа улсаас өөр улсад боловсруулж болно.

Таны сонголт

  • Зөвшөөрөл (Европын эдийн засгийн бүс, Их Британи, Швейцарь болон хуулиар шаардсан бусад бүс нутаг): анх нээхэд зөвшөөрлийн цонх гарна. Сонголтоо Тохиргоо → «Нууцлалын сонголт» хэсгээс хүссэн үедээ харж, өөрчилж болно (хуулиар шаардсан газарт л харагдана).
  • iOS: рекламын танигчийг мөрдлөгт ашиглахаас өмнө апп зөвшөөрөл асууна. Татгалзаж болно, эсвэл Тохиргоо → Нууцлал ба аюулгүй байдал → Tracking хэсгээс хожим өөрчилж болно. Тоглоом хэвээр ажиллах бөгөөд реклам хувийн тохируулгагүй болно.
  • Android: Тохиргоо → Нууцлал (эсвэл Google → Зар сурталчилгаа) хэсгээс рекламын ID-гаа шинэчлэх, хувийн тохируулгатай рекламаас татгалзах боломжтой.
